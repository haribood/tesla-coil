# Tesla Coil

A University of Houston ASME hardware project involving team-built Tesla coils, circuit assembly, and troubleshooting.

[Watch the Tesla coil demonstration](https://haribood.github.io/teslacoil1.mp4)

## Project overview

I helped lead a **20-member team** at the University of Houston to design and assemble **four Tesla coils over two days**. The project brought together hands-on circuit work, team coordination, and testing of high-voltage hardware.

## My contributions

- Helped lead and coordinate the team during the build.
- Supported soldering and circuit assembly.
- Assisted with troubleshooting the assembled circuits.
- Supported high-voltage safety during the project.

## Reported outcome

The team built and tested circuits producing approximately **10 cm plasma discharges**, as reported in my portfolio.

| Project detail | Result |
|----------------|--------|
| Organization | ASME, University of Houston |
| Team size | 20 members |
| Coils assembled | Four |
| Build period | Two days |
| Reported discharge length | Approximately 10 cm |
